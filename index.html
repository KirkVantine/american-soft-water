Systems we install and service

Every system is built and programmed for the water in your home, whether you’re on a private well or city water.

Water softeners

Hard water leaves scale in your pipes, water heater, and dishwasher, and it makes soap work harder. A softener swaps out the calcium and magnesium so fixtures stay clean and appliances last longer. We offer single-tank, twin-tank, and refurbished units.

  • Ends white crust and spotty dishes
  • Softer skin, hair, and laundry
  • Protects water heaters and appliances

Iron & sulfur filters

Well water in our area often carries iron and hydrogen sulfide. An air-injection filter holds a pocket of air in the tank that oxidizes iron and sulfur so the media can trap them. The system backwashes itself and refills its own air, with no chemicals to add.

  • Stops rust stains on sinks, tubs, and clothes
  • Removes the rotten-egg smell
  • Keeps iron out of your softener and plumbing

Reverse osmosis drinking water

An RO system pushes water through a pre-filter, a fine membrane, and a carbon filter, then stores it for a dedicated faucet at your sink. It’s the most thorough option for the water you drink and cook with.

  • Reduces lead, arsenic, nitrates, and dissolved solids
  • Removes chlorine taste and odor
  • Simple filter changes. We can do them for you.

Whole-house filtration

Some things a softener isn’t built to remove. A sediment or carbon pre-filter catches sand, grit, and chlorine before they reach the rest of the house, which also helps your softener last.

  • Clears sand, grit, and cloudiness
  • Carbon options for city chlorine
  • Arsenic removal units available

Common questions

How often should I test my water?

Once a year, especially if you’re on a well, and any time you notice a change in taste, smell, or staining.

Is the water test really free?

Yes. Dan tests at your home and explains the results on the spot. There’s no obligation to buy anything.

How long does a softener install take?

It depends on your plumbing and the system, but most installs are done the same day with only a short water shutoff.

Can I install a softener myself?

You can, but sizing and programming the valve for your water’s hardness and iron is where most DIY systems fall short. A professional install also keeps product and workmanship warranties intact.

How often does a softener need maintenance?

Keep the brine tank stocked with salt (we can deliver it), and have the system checked periodically. We clean resin, check salt levels, and confirm it’s regenerating correctly.

Do you repair systems you didn’t install?

Yes. Call with the brand and model if you have it, and Dan will tell you honestly whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense.
